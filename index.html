Meet Your Paediatrician

Academic Excellence Backed by
Compassionate Care

Dr. Pratheep Aynstin Antony completed his MBBS from Sri Manakula Vinayagar Medical College and Hospital with remarkable Distinction in 13 subjects, establishing an extraordinary academic foundation. He went on to pursue his MD in Paediatrics at Mahatma Gandhi Medical College and Research Institute (MGMCRI), graduating as the prestigious University Gold Medalist.

With one international publication to his credit, Dr. Pratheep has presented his clinical research at esteemed national medical conferences. He brings specialized training in NRP (Neonatal Resuscitation Program) and PALS (Pediatric Advanced Life Support), active experience in undergraduate and postgraduate medical teaching, and leadership in NICU quality-improvement initiatives.

Lactation counselling is specialized support for mothers experiencing breastfeeding difficulties—such as latch pain, cracked nipples, low milk supply anxiety, or infant colic. Dr. Pratheep provides hands-on posture guidance, assesses baby suck dynamics, and helps mothers nurse comfortably and confidently.
